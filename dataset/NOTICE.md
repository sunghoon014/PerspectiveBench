# Data license and attribution

Data under `dataset/`, including the samples, is released under [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](LICENSE).

PerspectiveBench was created by Sunghoon Kim and Sangyeop Kim. Source material from [OpenStax](https://openstax.org/) (Rice University) textbooks in Physics, Biology, and History was processed into factual evidence, reasoning paths, and perspective-targeted questions. Original source material retains its applicable license and attribution. The fictional samples were written for this repository.

When sharing adaptations, credit PerspectiveBench and the original sources, indicate your changes, and retain the applicable license notices.
